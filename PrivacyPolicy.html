Mastery Privacy Policy

Effective September 4, 2026

Overview

Mastery is designed to work without an account. The current version does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data-collection SDKs.

Information stored on your iPhone

Goals, descriptions, selected emoji, focus-session history, reflections, timer state, streaks, appearance choices, and notification preferences are stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to the developer or sold to anyone.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Mastery asks iOS for notification permission and schedules notifications on your device. You can turn reminders off inside Mastery or revoke permission in the iPhone Settings app.

Backups and exports

If you choose to export a backup or journal, Mastery writes your library information to a file at a location you choose, which may be a cloud file provider. Exported files contain your notes and remain there until you delete them separately. Deleting the app does not delete exported files.

Live Activities

An active timer can appear on the Lock Screen and other system surfaces. Goal titles are hidden by default and can be enabled in Mastery Settings. Reflections and session intentions are not included in the Live Activity.

Retention and deletion

Your information remains on your iPhone until you delete individual goals and their sessions or remove the app. Deleting the app removes Mastery’s locally stored data. Mastery does not maintain a server-side copy.

Sharing and tracking

Apart from files you choose to export, Mastery does not send personal information to third parties and does not track activity across other companies’ apps or websites.

Changes

If Mastery’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the changed functionality is released.
