Mastery Support

Mastery is a private, on-device focus timer and long-term goal tracker for iPhone.

Contact

For help, feedback, or feature requests, email soft_servd@icloud.com.

Notifications

Enable or disable reminders in Mastery under Settings → Notifications. If permission was previously denied, open the iPhone Settings app, select Mastery, and change Notifications there.

Deleting data

Open a goal and choose Delete to remove that goal and its tracked sessions. Removing Mastery from your iPhone deletes the app’s remaining locally stored data. Mastery does not keep a server copy.

Privacy

Mastery does not require an account and does not contain ads, analytics, or tracking. See the Mastery Privacy Policy for complete details.
